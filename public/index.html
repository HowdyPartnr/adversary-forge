Adversary Forge

I kept flipping between the SRD and a notes app every time I needed a monster halfway through a session, so I built this instead. Pick a role and a tier, get numbers that sit where the book says they should, then change whatever you want.

Role

What kind of thing is it?

This decides the shape of the block. Minions lose their thresholds, Solos pick up Relentless, that sort of thing.

Tier

Who is it fighting?

Follow your party. Everything below moves with it.

Identity

Name and motives

Motives and Tactics are just verbs, and they save you when you have no idea what the thing would do next. I lifted every suggestion here off the SRD's own adversaries and sorted them by role, so you get lines the game actually uses.

Stats

The numbers

Benchmark numbers with the role adjustment already applied. Push them wherever you like. The range under each one is a guide, not a wall.

Features

What can it actually do?

Every one of these is copied out of the SRD word for word, with the adversary it came from. I stopped rewriting them in my own words because a feature that says Restrained is no use if it never tells you how Restrained ends. Filtered to the role you picked.

Where this comes from. The feature library, the conditions, the ten roles, the attack ranges and the Motives and Tactics suggestions are all reproduced from the Daggerheart System Reference Document 1.0 as written, not summarised. Every feature is labelled with the adversary it belongs to. The tier benchmarks and ranges are the SRD's own numbers. The only thing here that is mine is how each role nudges those benchmarks, and that is a starting point, not a rule.
